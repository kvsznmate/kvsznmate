## About me

Hi, I'm **Mate Kovasznai**, a 3rd year Data Science & AI student at **[Breda University of Applied Sciences](https://www.buas.nl/)**.

My main areas are natural language processing, computer vision and data analysis. Right now I'm going deeper into the MLOps lifecycle, because I enjoy taking a model beyond the notebook: containerising it, deploying and measuring whether it actually works in practice.

Over the past few years I've built a wide range of machine learning projects. I created an end-to-end system that turns video and audio into per-sentence emotion predictions, developed a vision-guided robotics pipeline that inoculates plant root tips with a 0.5 mm error, and engineered a real-time transcription soundboard that responds to spoken words in under half a second. 

I've also trained a skin-disease classifier, built a cloud pipeline that predicts NO₂ levels around Breda, and worked hands-on with the Unitree Go2-W and G1 humanoid robots using ROS2, lidar and isaac-sim. 

As I mentioned right now I'm focused on production MLOps, statistics, and data storage tools and concepts,  I'm looking for a data science or ML engineering internship, or a data analyst or translator role, where I can ship and measure real systems. 

When I'm not in the lab or behind a screen, you'll probably find me playing chess ♟️
